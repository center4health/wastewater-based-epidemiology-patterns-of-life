Where do we poop? A city, seventeen ways.

Every agent in this simulation eats, works, socializes, and defecates on its own schedule. Change one number — the baseline transmission rate — and the wastewater signal the city produces changes with it. These are the 17 runs of that sweep, from 0.100 to 0.500.

Reading these plots

The epidemic curve counts agents whose exposure begins in each interval. The pathogen plot is the quantity that would reach a treatment plant — the wastewater-observable signal. Disease status tracks how the population divides into susceptible, exposed, infectious, and recovered.

Runs at the low end of the sweep die out early, so their animations are shorter than the rest.

Source

Amiri, Hossein, et al. Where do We Poop? City-Wide Simulation of Defecation Behavior for Wastewater-Based Epidemiology. arXiv preprint arXiv:2601.04231 (2026).
